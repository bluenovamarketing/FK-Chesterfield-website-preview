Common home plumbing problems

Know when it is time to call a plumber.

Small symptoms can point to a larger plumbing issue. Early diagnosis may help limit water damage, protect fixtures and prevent a minor problem from becoming an emergency.

Leaks & water damage

Drips, stains or rising water bills

A hidden pipe or fixture leak can waste water and damage cabinets, walls and floors. Call when a leak persists, returns after tightening a fitting or cannot be safely isolated.

Low water pressure

Weak flow throughout the house

Low pressure may come from a fixture, valve, supply pipe or water main issue. A plumber can narrow down whether the problem is isolated or affecting the whole home.

Drain trouble

Slow drains and repeat backups

Multiple slow fixtures, gurgling or sewage odors can signal a deeper line problem. Explore professional sewer and drain cleaning before the blockage worsens.

Hot water problems

No hot water or inconsistent temperature

Rust-colored water, popping sounds, visible tank moisture and frequent temperature changes are signs that your water heater needs professional attention.

Basement protection

A sump pump that will not run

A failed pump or backup system can leave your basement exposed during a storm or power outage. Testing and service before heavy rainfall can reduce that risk.

Winter plumbing

Frozen or vulnerable pipes

Little or no flow during freezing weather may mean a frozen pipe. Avoid open flames or unsafe thawing methods; call for controlled pipe-thawing help.

Water heater repair and replacement

Reliable hot water matters for bathing, cleaning and everyday comfort. Frost & Kretsch Plumbing services conventional gas and electric water heaters along with energy-saving tankless systems.

  • Diagnosis of no-hot-water and temperature problems
  • Repair or replacement recommendations based on system condition
  • Installation of properly sized tank and tankless equipment
  • Connections, venting and final operation checks
Call promptly: Active leaking around a water heater can damage nearby floors and walls. If safe, shut off the water supply and call (586) 675-0097.

Sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps

Below-grade plumbing relies on pumps to move water where gravity cannot. Service includes primary sump pumps, water-operated backup pumps and sewage ejector pumps for basement plumbing installations.

  • New pump installation and replacement
  • Float, discharge and check-valve troubleshooting
  • Backup protection for power outages
  • Ejector-pump support for basement bathrooms

If outside drainage or a damaged underground line is contributing to the problem, the team can also coordinate plumbing excavation and underground repair.

Do you provide emergency home plumbing service?

Yes. Emergency residential plumbing help is available 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays. Call (586) 675-0097 for current response availability.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Your technician diagnoses the problem, explains the recommended work and provides the project price before beginning the repair. The final bill will not exceed the quoted project price for the authorized work.

What types of water heaters do you service?

Frost & Kretsch Plumbing services gas and electric tank-style water heaters as well as water- and energy-saving tankless systems.

Can you help with recurring drain backups?

Yes. The team offers professional drain and sewer cleaning, high-pressure water jetting and video camera inspection to diagnose deeper line problems.

Do you handle permits for larger projects?

For water main, sewer connection and repiping projects, permit and insurance requirements can be coordinated on your behalf.

What should I do before an emergency plumber arrives?

If it is safe, shut off the nearest fixture valve or the home’s main water supply. Move belongings away from active water, avoid electrical hazards and do not use chemical drain cleaners while waiting for professional help.

Do you install customer-supplied fixtures?

Fixture compatibility and installation requirements vary. Call before purchasing so the team can discuss the fixture, existing connections and what may be needed for a proper installation.
