How quickly can a plumber come out?

Response time depends on your location, the type of plumbing problem and current call volume. Call (586) 675-0097 for current scheduling and emergency availability.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service after hours?

Yes. Frost & Kretsch Plumbing provides 24/7 emergency service for urgent residential and commercial plumbing problems, including weekends and holidays.

Can you give pricing over the phone?

Many plumbing problems require an on-site diagnosis before an accurate project price can be provided. Your technician explains the recommended work and pricing before the repair begins.

What should I do while waiting for a plumber?

If it is safe, shut off the water supply and move belongings away from the affected area. Avoid standing water near electrical equipment, and call immediately if the problem is causing active damage.

Do you handle drain, sewer and water heater problems too?

Yes. The team handles drain and sewer service, water heater repairs and replacements, general plumbing, commercial work and plumbing excavation.
